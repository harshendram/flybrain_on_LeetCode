What am I looking at?

These are the 78 neuropils of a female fruit fly, the JFRC2 template surfaces transformed into FlyWire space (Ito et al. 2014, via the FlyWire project). The faint shell is FlyWire's own whole-brain outline. Dots are the somas of 118,104 proofread neurons.

Click a region and the neurons drawn inside it are real cells from FlyWire v783, chosen as the ones with the most synapses there, each a different cell type. Spikes running along them are a visualisation, not a recording.

LeetFly's learning model uses three of these regions: the antennal lobe (the smell arrives), the mushroom body (Kenyon cells learn), and it leaves the lateral horn (innate smell) alone.

Meshes: Ito et al. 2014, transformed by the FlyWire / fafbseg project. Neurons and somas: FlyWire v783, Dorkenwald et al. and Schlegel et al. 2024, CC-BY 4.0. Not affiliated with LeetCode.
